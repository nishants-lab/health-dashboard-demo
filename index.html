Everyday habits, explained

Prewritten educational examples alongside the demo trends. These do not change with uploads and are not personalized treatment recommendations.

Food choices

A simple meal pattern can include vegetables, whole grains and a protein source such as lentils, beans, eggs or tofu. Choose options that fit your preferences and allergies.

Try tracking: meal regularity and food variety, to discuss alongside your lab history.

Movement

Choose activities you enjoy, such as walking, cycling or strength exercises appropriate to your abilities. Seek professional guidance if you have symptoms, medical conditions or activity restrictions.

Try tracking: activity type, duration and how you feel.

Sleep and routine

Keep a consistent sleep schedule where possible, and notice patterns in rest, energy and daily routines.

Try tracking: bedtimes and wake times, without assuming these explain a lab change.

Prepare for a consultation

Bring your original reports and relevant context, including symptoms and changes in prescribed medicines. Ask a clinician which changes matter for you.
